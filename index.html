About Jessica

Jessica Carter, PhD, RDN, LD, CDCES, FMCP

Registered dietitian, diabetes care and education specialist, functional medicine practitioner, lifestyle coach, and experienced registered yoga teacher.

Education

Nutrition, dietetics, and biology

Jessica earned an MS in Nutrition and Dietetics and a BS in Biology. Her LinkedIn profile lists Saybrook University.

Experience

Health care and nutrition
